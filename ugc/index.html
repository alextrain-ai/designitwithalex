Who you're hiring

Hi, I'm Alex

Rather hear it than read it? 33 seconds, with sound.

I'm 20, based in Prague, and I make short-form video every single day.

I didn't come to this from acting. I came from editing — building motion design and ads for brands, watching which frames people actually stayed for. Then I started making my own videos, posting one a day, and learned the harder half of the job: the first two seconds decide everything. You can have a perfect product and lose the viewer before you've said its name.

So that's what I obsess over. The hook. The pacing. The moment where a person decides to keep watching or flick away.

I shoot across fitness, tech, SaaS, travel and everyday-performance products — not because I haven't picked a lane, but because the same thing is being sold in all of them: a person you believe. A supplement, a focus tool, a client portal, a power bank when your phone hits 5% halfway across town. Different product, same two seconds deciding whether anyone stays.

I'm early in this and I'm not going to pretend otherwise. What I'll say instead: I shoot constantly, I study what works, and every video I make is better than the one before it. If you want someone who treats your product like their own reel — that's me.
